Fish Sort Puzzle Level 170 Board Notes

Fish Sort Puzzle Level 170 guide: compare three early orders, the lower-left chain and lower-center key, plus a 1:22 checkpoint for the changing board.

Original video unavailable: The Daisy Gaming upload tied to these historical observations can no longer be played. The player and VideoObject markup have been removed while a replacement is researched.

Level 170: reviewed board notes

These notes describe a visible checkpoint in the original Daisy Gaming recording, which is now unavailable, reviewed on 2026-10-02. Use them to compare your board. They are not a complete tap-by-tap solution; fish names below describe colors and shapes, not official species names.

The recorded progress counter has a goal of 183. This is the on-screen counter, not a move count or a count of fish visible in one frame.

Locate the chain, key, and three orders

At 0:10, the recording shows three active target portraits: a blue-and-yellow fish, an orange-striped fish, and a yellow-orange striped fish. A chain and gold lock marked 1 surround a group near the lower left. A gold key sits over the lower-center group beside two blue-faced, purple-finned fish. The counter goal is 183.

Do not treat the locked orange fish as a free third

Orange-striped fish appear both higher on the board and inside the chained lower-left group. Compare the unrestricted candidates first, and watch the key interaction before depending on a chained fish. Also separate the blue-and-yellow order portrait from the yellow-faced, blue-striped design: both use yellow and blue, but their face colors and markings differ.

Use the 1:22 frame to check a changed board

At 1:22, the displayed counter reads 52/183. The left jar holds two blue-faced purple-finned fish, the right jar holds two orange-striped fish, and another chained group is visible near the upper center. This is a mid-run comparison point, not the starting layout. If your orders or holding bar differ here, replay the preceding clear before copying the next taps.

Video source and version check

These Level 170 observations were originally reviewed from Daisy Gaming. That video is unavailable as of October 28, 2026. The written checkpoint remains as historical board evidence and is not presented as a playable or tested full solution.

The guide covers the Shycheese mobile game identified by Android package triple.sorting.bubble.fish.match; it is separate from the YIGOT browser game on the homepage.
